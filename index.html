Tap Copy save code anytime — it copies a short code to your clipboard. Paste it into Notes or a text to yourself. If you ever lose this page, reopen the board and tap Restore from code.

How this works

Tap once = someone else drafted them (grays out).
Tap again = you drafted them (fills your roster).
Tap a third time = resets back to available.

Your picks now save automatically to this browser. The save code is your backup — use it if you ever switch devices or clear your browser data.
